A little context

A short history of Mabini

Mabini is named for Apolinario Mabini, the Filipino revolutionary and statesman born in Batangas. Before becoming a separate municipality, the area was part of Bauan.

01 JANUARY 1918

The Municipality of Mabini was created on January 1, 1918, with Don Francisco Castillo as its first appointed municipal president. The town was named in honor of Apolinario Mabini.
